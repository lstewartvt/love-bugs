Our Story

Created for families who want more

Love Bugs Learning Home was created for low-income families who want more than affordable childcare… they want a place where their child is loved, guided, and prepared.

We're a home-based learning environment designed to feel safe and familiar while still offering the structure and educational focus children need to grow.

Our goal is simple: help every child build a strong foundation, academically and socially, through daily routines, meaningful learning, and joyful play.

Meet the Caregiver

Ms. Alexis Patterson

Your Child's Early Learning Guide

Primary Caregiver • 7+ Years Experience

Ms. Alexis is the primary caregiver and early-learning guide at Love Bugs Learning Home. With 7+ years of experience across daycare, nannying, and child care settings, she brings both warmth and strong classroom structure to every day.

Her focus includes:

  • Helping children build early academic skills in age-appropriate ways
  • Supporting social-emotional development (confidence, empathy, communication)
  • Creating consistent routines that make children feel safe and capable
  • Partnering with families through open communication and trust

Our Philosophy

We believe children learn best when they feel...

🛡️

Safe

Predictable routine, calm guidance, and consistent boundaries create security.

👀

Seen

Small group, individual attention, and understanding each child's unique needs.

🌱

Challenged

Learning through play, questions, exploration, and age-appropriate practice.

🎉

Celebrated

Encouragement, confidence-building, and positive reinforcement every day.

We focus on the whole child: mind, body, and heart.

For Families

What families can expect

📚 A learning-focused day

Early literacy, early math, language development, fine motor skills, and creative thinking are woven into play and daily activities.

💕 Social skills taught with care

We coach children through real moments: sharing, waiting, using words, managing big feelings, and building friendships.

🏡 A respectful, family-centered environment

You'll always know what your child is working on, how they're adjusting, and how we can support them together. We also offer camera subscription access for you to view your child throughout the day.
