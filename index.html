Our Approach

Play-based learning with intentional education

At Love Bugs Learning Home, children thrive in a small-group environment where they're known, supported, and encouraged every day. Ms. Alexis blends play-based learning with intentional early education.

Building Skills That Matter

What children learn at Love Bugs

📖Early literacy & language (letters, sounds, vocabulary, storytelling)
🔢Early math & thinking (counting, patterns, problem-solving)
💚Social-emotional growth (sharing, empathy, self-control, confidence)
🎒Kindergarten readiness habits (routines, listening, independence)
🌟Ages 5+: After-school learning retention and cool down

Why Choose Us
