A FCCLH is a Georgia-licensed home-based child care program with a small group setting. Love Bugs Learning Home provides care for 6 or fewer children, allowing for more individualized attention and a calm, consistent routine.

We typically serve children ages 5 weeks–5+ years. If you're unsure, reach out—placement depends on current openings and age mix.

We are open:

  • Day Care: Weekdays 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • After-School Child Care: Weekdays 5:00 AM – 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM
  • Overnight Child Care: Weekdays 10:00 PM – 7:00 AM; Weekends available upon request

Early drop-off/late pick-up options may be available based on scheduling and availability.

We are located in Decatur/Snapfinger, DeKalb County, Georgia. The exact address and tour details are shared after an initial phone call or message.

Enrollment is simple:

  1. Request Enrollment Info
  2. Schedule a Tour / Meet & Greet (optional)
  3. Review and sign the parent packet + policies
